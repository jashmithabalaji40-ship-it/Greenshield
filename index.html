Small habits, stronger protection

Use these practical steps every day.

🔐

Keep passwords and OTPs private

Use unique passwords and never share one-time codes, even with someone claiming to be support.

⬇

Install apps from trusted stores

Avoid unknown APK files and review app permissions before installing or updating apps.

🔗

Check links before opening

Look closely at the domain, be cautious of urgent messages, and visit known official sites directly.

🛡

Turn on two-factor authentication

Use an authenticator or passkey where available, and keep recovery options up to date.

↻

Update your phone

Install operating-system and app updates to receive security fixes.

📶

Be careful on public Wi-Fi

Avoid sensitive tasks on untrusted networks and never ignore browser security warnings.

Learn About Phone Hacking

Phone compromise can happen in several ways. Knowing the common warning signs can help you respond safely.

✉

Phishing

Fake messages or websites try to trick people into giving away passwords, OTPs, or financial information.

📱

Malicious apps

Untrusted apps may request excessive permissions or collect information they do not need.

👤

Social engineering

Scammers use urgency, fear, or impersonation to persuade someone to reveal information.

⚙

Unsafe settings

Outdated software, weak screen locks, and unnecessary permissions can increase risk.

What should you do if you suspect a problem?

  1. Do not reply, pay, or share codes with the sender.
  2. Use a trusted device to change affected passwords and secure important accounts.
  3. Remove apps you do not recognise and review permissions.
  4. Contact your bank or service provider through its official channel if an account may be affected.
