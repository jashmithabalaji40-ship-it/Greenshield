CYBER HYGIENE GUIDE

Security Tips & Threat Education

Practical knowledge to recognize and neutralize social engineering attacks.

1. Phishing & Fake Websites

Always scrutinize domain spelling. Attackers use rn instead of m (e.g. arnazon.com) or lookalike international characters. Real banks will never demand password changes via SMS links.

2. OTP & Password Scams

Never share an OTP over a phone call, even if the caller displays your bank’s real number on Caller ID. Banks have internal authorization and will never call you asking for your one-time code.

3. Suspicious APK Files

Never install .apk files received via WhatsApp, Telegram, or SMS claiming to be "Loan Approvals", "PAN Card Verification", or "Speed Post Trackers". These are remote-access trojans (RATs).

4. Public Wi-Fi Risks

Avoid logging into bank accounts on open Wi-Fi at airports or cafes without a VPN. Hackers run rogue "Evil Twin" hotspots with identical names to sniff unencrypted data packets.
